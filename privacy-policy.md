# Privacy Policy — Prime Draw Gaming

**Effective Date:** October 26, 2026
**Last Updated:** October 26, 2026

Thank you for using **Prime Draw Gaming** (the "App"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal information.

---

> ⚠️ **Disclaimer:** Prime Draw Gaming is a real-money lottery gaming app. It is intended only for users aged **18 or older** in places where taking part is legal. Please play responsibly.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

To run your account, wallet and games, we collect:

- Email address or username
- Name
- Phone number (optional)
- Profile photo (optional)
- Passcode (stored only in encrypted form — we cannot read it)
- Deposit and withdrawal requests, payment receipt images and bank QR code images
- Bets you place (lottery, draw time, numbers and amounts)
- Support chat messages and files you send
- Feedback and screenshots you submit
- IP address and device information when you log in or out

We do **NOT** collect:

- GPS/location data
- Contacts
- Payment card details

---

## 2. What the App Does

**Prime Draw Gaming** lets you play lottery games with a wallet balance: you can deposit funds, place bets, view results, receive winnings, request withdrawals and chat with our support team.

---

## 3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers, and uploaded files (receipts, QR codes, photos, chat files) are stored with our cloud storage provider.

- Bets, transactions and login history are automatically deleted after **2 months** (pending deposit and withdrawal requests are kept until they are processed).
- Your wallet balance is kept for as long as your account exists.
- Account details, support chat and feedback are kept while your account is active, or until you ask us to delete them.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

We do **not** sell your personal information. We use these trusted services to run the App:

- **DigitalOcean** — stores uploaded files
- **Pusher** — delivers real-time chat replies and results
- **Cloudflare** — protects and speeds up our website and API
- **Telegram** — sends deposit and withdrawal alerts (user ID, amount and receipt image) to our staff for processing

Branch administrators can see your account, wallet, bets, login history and support chat so they can process payments and help you. We may also share information when required by law.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these third-party services.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

This app is **not** intended for anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us and we will close the account and remove their information.

---

## 6. Your Rights

- View and update your name, phone number, profile photo and passcode in the App
- Log out at any time to end your session on that device
- Request deletion of your account and data by contacting us (we may keep records needed to finish pending payments or meet legal obligations)
